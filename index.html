about

A bit of background

I'm from Rwanda and I've been writing software for a few years now, starting with small personal web projects and moving into building and running my own company. I studied Software Development at Runda TSS, and I'm now studying Software Engineering at INES Ruhengeri.

In 2025 I founded Oliviuus Ltd, where I'm CEO and CTO. iSanzure, a platform for finding and renting real estate in Rwanda, is built under it. Since 2023 I've also been co-running Linkfy Connect, a platform for connecting people and opportunities.

Day to day I work mostly in JavaScript and Python, using Node and Express or Flask on the backend, React and Next.js on the front end, and MySQL or MongoDB depending on the project. I'm also interested in applied AI and try to bring it into what I build when it actually solves something, not just because it's trendy.
